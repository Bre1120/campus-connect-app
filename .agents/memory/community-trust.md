---
name: Community trust boundaries
description: Why peer-help identities and message reports must not be presented as verified or actively moderated.
---

Community helpers may describe themselves as tutors or advisors, but this is not a verified professional directory. Do not imply credentials, employment, availability, or guaranteed responses without an actual vetting and staffing process.

**Why:** The user wanted students to speak with both peers and tutors/advisors, but no trusted roster or credential verification workflow existed. Overstating identity would mislead students.

**How to apply:** Keep self-described labels visible anywhere helper roles appear. If verification is added, build a real admin approval and revocation flow before changing the language. Message reports are stored, not actively reviewed; do not promise review or resolution until a staffed moderation workflow exists.
