name: Navigation preference
description: User-requested direction to preserve during future Campus Connect redesigns.
---

The user requested navigation in a footer instead of the header-side panel, with only three footer tabs. All tasks and Study belong together; Profile, Settings, and Sign out belong together.

**Why:** This is the user's requested layout, not an incidental generated design choice.

**How to apply:** Preserve three primary footer tabs and the requested groupings in future app-shell redesigns unless the user asks for a different layout. Keep all app and account destinations accessible through their groups on small screens, and reserve content space so the footer does not cover controls.
