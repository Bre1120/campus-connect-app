App Idea:
Campus Connect is an academic organization app designed to help college students keep track of assignments, exams, projects, and other important deadlines in one place. The app should give students a simple way to see what they need to complete, when it is due, and what they have already finished.
Target User:
College students, primarily ages 18–25, who are taking multiple courses and need an easier way to organize their assignments, exams, projects, and academic deadlines.
Core Features:
1.	Assignment and Deadline Tracker – Students can add assignments, exams, projects, and other tasks along with the course name, due date, and due time.
2.	Personalized Dashboard – Students can view today's tasks, upcoming deadlines, and their weekly completion progress from one main dashboard.
3.	Reminders and Task Completion – Students can receive reminders about upcoming deadlines and mark assignments or tasks as completed.
Pages Needed:
•	Dashboard/Home – Displays today's tasks, upcoming deadlines, and weekly progress.
•	Calendar – Shows assignments, exams, projects, and deadlines organized by date.
•	Tasks – Displays all academic tasks and allows students to add, edit, delete, or mark them as completed.
•	Profile – Contains basic student information and preferences.
•	Settings – Allows students to manage notifications, reminders, and other app preferences.
Data Source (Optional):
Use a simple database to store each user's courses, assignments, exams, projects, due dates, and task completion status.
Authentication:
Email and password login with the option to create a personal student account.
Design Style:
Clean, modern, simple, and mobile-first. The design should use an organized dashboard with easy-to-read cards for assignments and deadlines. Important upcoming deadlines should be easy to identify. Navigation should be simple so students can quickly move between the Dashboard, Calendar, Tasks, Profile, and Settings pages.
Integrations (Optional):
Calendar integration could be added so students can sync academic deadlines with their personal calendar. Future versions could potentially connect with college learning management systems to automatically import assignments and deadlines.
Deployment:
A mobile-friendly web app that works well on smartphones, tablets, and computers. The app should be responsive so students can access their academic information from different devices.
